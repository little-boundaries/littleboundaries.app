DEVICE BOUNDARIES + APP SETTINGS

A few layers.
A stronger foundation.

Little Boundaries is a Mac app in development that helps parents configure supported restrictions on an iPhone or iPad. Those device boundaries are one part of setup.

App-level parental controls are a separate step. Little Boundaries does not turn on these services’ family tools, manage social accounts, or read messages. For apps you choose to allow, review their own privacy, contact, content, and time settings too.

  1. Set up the device.Start with Family Sharing and Screen Time, then add supported device restrictions if your family needs them. Follow the device setup guide →
  2. Choose the apps together.Check the service’s minimum age and local requirements. Use the correct birthday; family tools do not make an underage account appropriate.
  3. Connect the family tools.Pick an app below, complete its official setup, and check the result on your child’s account. Revisit it as they grow.

Start with the apps your child already uses. Many services need a parent account and your child’s participation. Features vary by age, country, and app version; follow the current official guide if the screens differ.

Social & sharing

Teen Accounts & supervision

Instagram

Start with Meta’s guide to Teen Accounts, then connect supervision through Family Center. Review account privacy, messaging settings, and daily time limits.

Before you start: Have your teen join you to complete the supervision invitation. Device restrictions do not connect these accounts for you.

Instagram’s parent guide ↗

Family Pairing

TikTok

Link your parent and teen accounts through Family Pairing. Review time limits, Restricted Mode, and the privacy and messaging options available for your teen.

Before you start: You’ll need to complete account pairing together. Available controls depend on your teen’s age and region.

TikTok’s Family Pairing setup ↗

Family Center

Snapchat

Use Snapchat’s setup walkthrough to connect Family Center and get insight into your teen’s friends, recent contacts, and time spent in the app.

Before you start: You need your own Snapchat account, must add each other as friends, and your teen must accept the invitation. Private conversation contents are not shown.

Snapchat’s Family Center setup ↗

Teen safety & parental passcode

Pinterest

Review Pinterest’s teen safety options together, including profile visibility, messaging, and the parental passcode for selected account settings.

Before you start: This guide explains teen settings and passcode protection; it is a different setup from a linked Family Center dashboard.

Pinterest’s teen safety guide ↗

Video & communities

Family Center & teen supervision

YouTube

Follow YouTube’s instructions to link parent and teen accounts in Family Center. Read what the teen supervision tools share with parents before connecting.

Before you start: Teen supervision is voluntary and either person can remove it. For younger children, use the separate supervised kid account setup below.

YouTube’s teen supervision setup ↗YouTube setup for younger children ↗

Family Center

Discord

Connect Family Center to review recent activity and manage supported settings for sensitive media, friend requests, and direct messages from server members.

Before you start: Your teen must accept the connection. Activity summaries do not show message contents; review the settings and eligibility in Discord’s guide.

Discord’s parent setup guide ↗

Games with social features

Parental controls

Roblox

Start with Roblox’s overview, then choose linked parent account controls or Parent mode where available. Review content, chat, spending, and time settings supported for your child.

Before you start: Controls change as children get older. Some spending and screen time controls no longer apply from age 13; older teens have fewer parent-managed settings.

Roblox’s parental controls guide ↗

Parental controls & PIN

Fortnite / Epic Games

Use Epic’s parental controls guide to review friend requests, voice and text chat, content ratings, and purchases. Set a parental controls PIN as instructed.

Before you start: Epic settings and device or console settings are separate. Review both, especially if your child plays on more than one platform.

Epic’s parental controls guide ↗

A QUICK CHECK BEFORE YOU FINISH

Try the settings together.

  • Confirm the correct child’s account is connected and that the intended controls are enabled.
  • Review who can contact them, who can see posts or location, and how to block or report someone.
  • Try a limit or approval request and check what each of you sees.
  • Agree on when to revisit the settings, including after birthdays, new accounts, or major app updates.

These tools can help reduce risk. They do not guarantee safety, filter every interaction, or replace regular conversations. A parent guide is a resource for an app your family chooses to use, not a recommendation to install it.

All setup links lead to the service’s own resources. Little Boundaries is independent of the services listed here. Their settings and eligibility can change.
