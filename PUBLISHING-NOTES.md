# Statements about signing and notarization

Checked against Apple’s published guidance on November 3, 2026.

Describe a verified build’s code-signing and notarization status factually. Do not describe Little Boundaries as Apple-approved, Apple-certified, security-verified by Apple, or malware-checked by Apple. Developer Program License Agreement section 5.3 specifically restricts representations about Apple performing security checks/malware detection or reviewing/approving an app for notarization. Avoid guarantees of safety or feature effectiveness based on notarization.

Keep the independence and trademark acknowledgments. This note concerns publishing language, not a full legal review.

Sources:
- https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/ (section 5.3)
- https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html (compatibility and endorsement/sponsorship)
- https://developer.apple.com/programs/ (Mac distribution outside the Mac App Store)
