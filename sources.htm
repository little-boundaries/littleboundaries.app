Three things that work together

Family Sharing connects separate accounts. Screen Time provides Apple’s everyday parental controls. A restrictions profile adds supported device settings. None guarantees safety, and a profile doesn’t create your family group or automatically enable Apple’s controls.

The family setup and Screen Time references were checked on November 3, 2026. Check Apple’s guidance again when setting up or updating your devices.
